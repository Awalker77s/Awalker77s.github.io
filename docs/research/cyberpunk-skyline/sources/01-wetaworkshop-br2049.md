# Wētā Workshop — Blade Runner 2049 project page

- URL: https://www.wetaworkshop.com/projects/bladerunner-2049
- Type: first-party studio (Weta Workshop built the film's physical miniatures)
- Fetched: 2026-08-31

## Load-bearing quotes

> "the Los Angeles of 2049 is populated with megalithic buildings on a scale unconceivable in today's world."

Three main miniature environments built:
1. Los Angeles cityscape with an L.A.P.D. tower
2. "The Trash Mesa" — "a vast ruin on the outskirts of L.A."
3. Wallace Towers — pyramid-shaped skyscrapers constructed around steel frames

- ~38 structures total; L.A.P.D. building was the largest single miniature at 4.5 m tall; Trash Mesa built at 87th scale.
- Design combined "precise 3D modelling and laser-cutting techniques with traditional model-making craftsmanship."
- Scale note: miniatures "even shrunk to as little as 600th scale, they dwarfed the artists and technicians who worked on them."
- Trash Mesa elements "crafted at Wētā Workshop then assembled on site like a model kit of enormous proportions."

## Relevance
Confirms: pyramid-shaped megatower (Wallace Towers) as the signature "hero" silhouette; "megalithic" scale language distinct from ordinary towers; a ruined/derelict mass (Trash Mesa) as a second distinct silhouette type (irregular debris massing, not a tower at all).
