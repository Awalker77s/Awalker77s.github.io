## Load-bearing quotes

> CTBUH defines supertall buildings as "more than 300 m (984 ft) in height."

> Height measurement methodology: "…lowest, significant, open-air, pedestrian entrance to the architectural top of the building, including spires, but not including antennae, signage, flagpoles or other functional-technical equipment."

## Relevance
Q2: establishes the real-world height threshold and — more usefully for a silhouette artist — the official distinction that a **spire counts as part of the building's silhouette/height** while an **antenna does not**. That is a direct, load-bearing design cue: our existing "signal spire" (a thin free-floating antenna line, engine.ts ~L755-770) is architecturally the wrong element to signify a supertall — a tapered spire integrated into the tower's own mass reads as "supertall," a thin antenna reads as "regular tower with a mast bolted on."
