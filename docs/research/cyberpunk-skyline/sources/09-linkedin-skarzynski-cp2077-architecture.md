## Relevance
Q1: confirms a *two-tier* structure vocabulary distinct from "many similar towers" — (1) uniform megablock clusters (Metabolism-inspired, repetitive modular mass) as background city fabric, and (2) a single material-differentiated hero tower (Arasaka: smooth black steel/glass) that dominates NOT purely through height but through material/color contrast with its surroundings. This corroborates and extends the anchor doc's BR2049 "1-2 oversized pyramid masses" finding: differentiation can come from shape AND from a distinct fill/material treatment, which is directly portable to a baked-sprite fill-color technique.
