## Load-bearing quotes

> Location scout Higami Haruhiku "meticulously photographed the [Kowloon Walled City] area before it was demolished and dissolved into the rest of Kowloon in 1993."

> Curator Stefan Riekeles on the artistic goal: "The idea was to evoke a feeling of submerging into the deep levels of the city, where a flood of information overflows the human senses and a lot of noise surrounds the people."

> Production process: Higami's black-and-white photographs served as architectural blueprints for existing environments; layout designers made line drawings; Ogura refined with color studies from his own location photography. For fictional areas, designer Takashi Watabe "functioned as the primary architect, inventing buildings and scenes without photographic reference."

> Resulting character: "dusty, steroid-pumped brutalism by day, and rain-drenched neon by night."
