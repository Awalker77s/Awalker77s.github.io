# The Skyscraper Museum — "Slenderness" (New York's Super Slenders)

- URL: https://skyscraper.org/super-slenders/slenderness/
- Type: authoritative industry/museum source (The Skyscraper Museum, NYC)
- Fetched: 2026-08-31

## Load-bearing quotes

> "Structural engineers generally consider skyscrapers with a minimum 1:10 or 1:12 ratio (of the width of the building's base to its height) to be 'slender.'"

> World Trade Center North Tower: "at a height of 1,368 feet and with a big square floor plate, 209 feet on each side, the ratio of its base to height was less than 1:7." (i.e. a "normal" supertall, NOT read as slender)

> 432 Park Avenue: "The base of the apartment building is 93 feet square, and the shaft will rise to 1,396 feet, making its slenderness ratio 1:15."

> Most extreme example: 111 W. 57th Street reaches "an extraordinary 1:23" ratio.

> Key distinction: "Tall and BIG are not the same thing" — height ranking does not correlate with floor area among slender towers.
