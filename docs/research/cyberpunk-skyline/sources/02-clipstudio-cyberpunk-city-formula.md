## Load-bearing quotes

> "Cables, pipes and layered structures that create visual complexity."

> "Skyscrapers exceeding dozens of floors, oversized vehicle components, and massive architectural forms." (scale emphasis)

> "Artificial light acts as the primary mood setter in cyberpunk environments, often coming from neon signs, billboards, and screens."

> "Overlapping building forms with limited detail to merge structures into a continuous skyline." (distant-mass technique)

> "Keep visual noise under control, and make sure every element in your landscape serves a purpose, not just decoration."

> "Too much light does not automatically create drama, and adding more objects or visual elements does not guarantee that a cyberpunk landscape will communicate its idea clearly."

> Core principle: focus on "composition, lighting, and atmosphere instead of getting lost in unnecessary structural details."
